# 🏭 AeroHome Manufacturing – Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Manufacturing Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze production, quality, finance, procurement, supply chain, and workforce performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

The AeroHome Manufacturing Dashboard provides a centralized view of manufacturing operations through interactive **KPIs, charts, trends, filters, and business insights**.

It helps management monitor operational performance and identify areas for improvement.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

- 🏠 **Executive Overview** – Overall manufacturing KPIs and performance
- 🏭 **Production & Plant Performance** – Plant-wise production and target analysis
- 🛡️ **Quality & Operational Efficiency** – Defects, quality, and efficiency analysis
- 💰 **Finance & Cost Analysis** – Cost and financial performance
- 📦 **Procurement & Supplier Performance** – Supplier and procurement analysis
- 🚚 **Raw Materials & Supply Chain Risk** – Material and supply chain risk analysis
- 👷 **Operator & Shift Performance** – Operator productivity and shift analysis
