# 🏥 MediCare Healthcare Operations & Revenue Analytics

## Overview

MediCare is a **SQL / MySQL Healthcare Analytics Project** focused on analyzing hospital operations, patient services, clinical activities, and financial performance.

The project uses relational database analysis to identify operational trends, monitor healthcare service utilization, and evaluate billing and payment collection performance.

## Objectives

- Analyze hospital operations and patient activity
- Evaluate department performance and doctor workload
- Monitor appointment and admission trends
- Analyze room occupancy and resource utilization
- Evaluate treatment, laboratory, and pharmacy activities
- Track billing, revenue, and payment collections
- Identify outstanding payments and revenue collection gaps
- Generate meaningful SQL-based business insights

## Technology Stack

- **Database:** MySQL

## SQL Analysis

The project applies SQL techniques to extract, transform, validate, and analyze healthcare data.

- `select`, `where`, `order by`, `limit`
- Aggregate functions: `sum()`, `avg()`, `count()`
- Grouping and filtering: `group by`, `having`
- Joins: `inner join`, `left join`, `right join`
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window functions: `row_number()`, `rank()`
- Analytical functions: `lag()`, `lead()`

## Financial Analysis

The financial analysis focuses on understanding hospital revenue and payment performance through billing and payment records.

- Calculate total billed revenue
- Measure total payment collections
- Track paid and pending payment statuses
- Analyze payment methods
- Identify outstanding balances and collection gaps
- Validate billing and payment relationships to prevent duplicate counting

## Project Outcome

MediCare transforms healthcare operational and financial data into **meaningful KPIs, SQL-driven insights, and dashboard-ready outputs**. These insights can help hospital management understand service utilization, monitor financial performance, and make data-driven operational decisions.
